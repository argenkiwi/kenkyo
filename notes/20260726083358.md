To allow space-based chords (such as d+space) in Kanata, the space key (`spc`) must be mapped to the chord engine (e.g., `(chord mhbc spc)`) in the active layout, and its individual tap/hold or fork behavior must be defined under the `defchords` block as `(spc) ...`.
