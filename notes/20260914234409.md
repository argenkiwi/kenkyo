The bilateral cross-hand space macro chords (D+K, X+dot, V+M) were replaced with adjacent same-hand pairs always requiring Space: S+D+Space, C+V+Space, X+C+Space (left); K+L+Space, M+comma+Space, comma+dot+Space (right). Rationale: bilateral pairs conflicted ambiguously with individual one-shot modifier chords (D+Space, K+Space etc.), and the no-Space variants were redundant since Space is definitionally part of the chord. Applied symmetrically in both keyd/default.conf and kanata/kanata.kbd.
