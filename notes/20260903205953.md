The Shift space-macro chords were changed from C+Space (left) and comma+Space (right) to E+Space and I+Space. E is above D/Shift on the left hand; I is above K/Shift on the right. The kanata implementation required merging the former mtl (w/e/esc) and mtr (i/o/bspc) chord groups into mhbc, since a key can only belong to one chord group per layer and e/i were previously exclusive to mtl/mtr. All w/e/i/o keys in deflayermap (main) now point to mhbc. README section 8 updated accordingly.
