Kenkyo layout features are sequenced in order of increasing cognitive load and mechanical complexity: 1. One-key chords (base navigation), 2. SpaceFN/Extend (intuitive layer hold), 3. Home/Bottom row mods (dual-role hold), 4. Fumbol layer (specialized layout), 5. One-shot modifier mechanics (single-key, space-anchoring, multi-modifiers, and bilateral space macros).
