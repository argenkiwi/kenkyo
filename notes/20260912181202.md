Replaced unilateral top/bottom row space macro chords (e+space, i+space, etc.) with symmetric bilateral modifier pairs (d+k for Shift, x+. for AltGr, v+m for Fumbol) triggered either directly or with space. This unifies space macro chords across hands without relying on extra top-row anchor keys.
