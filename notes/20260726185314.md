Replaced the legacy E/R layer swaps on the Extend layer with space-anchored one-shot modifier chords. Tapping a modifier key (Shift, Fumbol, AltGr) with Space triggers a one-shot modifier, and keeping Space held down after releasing the other key keeps the modifier active. This approach also enables anchoring the AltGr modifier.
